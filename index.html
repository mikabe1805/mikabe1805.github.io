About me

I’m studying electrical and computer engineering at Rutgers, with a minor in computer science. I’m especially interested in applied AI: building the software around a model that turns its capability into something useful.

My work moves between research and products. I’ve led manuscript and reproducibility work for an 18-member accessibility research team, led an interactive breadboard simulator, and built two apps around everyday needs. Course and project work also keeps me close to hardware, from digital logic and circuit simulation to ESP32 sensor systems.

Outside of engineering, I’m usually thinking about story games and what makes a character or an experience stay with someone. That attention to the person on the other side shows up in my software, too.
