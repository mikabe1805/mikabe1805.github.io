Room of Days

A free iPhone habit app built like a life RPG. Everyday tasks become Quests that earn XP, raise six life stats and furnish an illustrated room. I came up with it, built it as the only developer and took it through Apple review; it has been on the App Store since August 19, 2026.

What finishing a Quest does

One tap closes the ring and sends the XP up a gold thread to the XP number. A receipt then lists what changed: XP and Glimmers, the life stat, the streak multiplier and how far the next level is. The board below is the app’s Quest board rebuilt for the browser, with its own timings, sounds and numbers.

One completion, in the app’s timing

  1. 0 msThe ring closes and the check draws in with the 460 ms completion sound, and a gold thread carries the XP up to the XP number.
  2. 240 msThe receipt rises. Its chips arrive 85 ms apart, and the stat and streak chips each play a note.
  3. 520 msXP, Glimmers and the stat are applied. The XP number rolls up over 650 ms and the bar steps to its new length. The finished card becomes a compact row, and swipe to undo is ready.
  4. 720 msOn the last of today’s three, a wash of embers and the streak sound.
  5. about 4 sThe receipt fades. If the XP crossed a level, the level-up takes over after it.
  6. Level upA full takeover: the new level slams onto a diamond, and one tap returns to the board.

A missed day never costs progress

Stats never decay. Showing up earns streak freezes, one every two active days at Care 40 or more, and a freeze quietly holds a missed day. A return after a longer gap pays a ×1.5 welcome back.

Six rooms

From the App Store listing

  1. Today’s Quests. Six life stats sit above the Choose today prompt and the day’s open Quests; Read ten pages is the main Quest at +26 XP.

  2. A finished Quest. +26 XP, +8 Glimmers and +6 Mind land together, the streak multiplier shows ×1.6, and the receipt offers swipe to undo.

  3. The Workshop. The Steward, an optional character, asks what you’re working on. His lines are pre-written, with no AI chat.

  4. When the day changes. A sheet offers to make the current move smaller, prepare the return, or leave today alone, which keeps your proof and current Quest where they are.

  5. Plans. The Daybook calendar, with the next class above the month view.

  6. My Space. A private page where each section is labeled with who can see it; here every section is set to Only you.

  7. Change Space. The Living Conservatory preview with Move In; the app notes that switching rooms never costs anything again.

  8. Journal. Today’s Quest thread (+26 XP, Mind +6) attaches automatically when you write, and older entries show the player title they were written under beside the current one.

  9. Discover. An optional directory of open rooms; only a public name, the room’s title, the room and the level appear.

How it’s built

Offline first
The save on the phone is the source of truth. The core app works with no account, and Firebase backup is optional.
A protected social layer
Shared rooms and an opt-in directory run on TypeScript Cloud Functions that filter public names, rate-limit reports and support blocking. Protected writes require Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest, and calls without attestation are rejected on the live deployment.
Release pipeline
Codemagic CI tests, signs and uploads each iOS build with a written release record: builds up to 49 across versions 1.0.0 to 1.0.4.
Native widget
The latest builds add a Swift WidgetKit Home Screen widget beside the Flutter app.
Sound and music
In-key interaction sounds tuned on an iPhone speaker, and an original soundtrack synthesized in code.

My part

I came up with Room of Days and checked the idea against competing apps and habit research before building it. I own its product direction, game design, UX, testing with family and outside testers, App Store submission, privacy answers and moderation.

I set the art direction for room art made with an image model. The soundtrack is synthesized by code written to my brief, and I chose the takes by ear. The app itself runs no AI.
